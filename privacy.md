# Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 October 2026.

This policy covers the applications and sites under `kfirs.com` (the "Service"), run by Arik Kfir.

## What we collect

- **Account details**: when you sign in with a third-party provider such as Google or GitHub, your name, email address and the identifier the provider gives us.
- **Usage data**: standard server logs, such as IP address, browser, requested pages and timestamps.

## How we use it

Only to sign you in, run and secure the Service, and fix problems. We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

## Sharing

We share data only with the infrastructure providers that host the Service (such as Google Cloud), or when the law requires it.

## Retention

We keep data while your account is in use, and logs only as long as needed to run and secure the Service.

## Your choices

You can ask to see or delete your data at any time, and you can revoke the Service's access from your sign-in provider's account settings.

## Changes

We may update this policy. The date above shows the latest version.
